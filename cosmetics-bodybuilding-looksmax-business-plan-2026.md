# Business Plan (2026-2029)
# Performance Aesthetics Lab
## Cosmetics + Bodybuilding + Looksmaxing

---

## 1) Executive Summary

Performance Aesthetics Lab is a compliance-first consumer health and beauty company focused on the intersection of:
- **Cosmetic performance** (skin, anti-aging, appearance quality),
- **Body performance** (strength, recovery, body composition),
- **Looks optimization** (grooming, hair/scalp, confidence-driven routines).

The business model combines:
1. **Topical cosmetics** (high repeat purchase, lower regulatory burden),
2. **Sports/bodybuilding supplements** (high velocity, high AOV bundles),
3. **Evidence-led looksmax products** (male and unisex grooming ecosystems).

We avoid high-risk injectable/gray-market peptide pathways and instead build a durable, legal, scalable brand with strong product quality and disciplined claims.

### Core objective
Reach profitable multi-market operation by Year 3 through DTC + marketplace distribution, subscription retention systems, and selective geographic expansion.

---

## 2) Problem and Opportunity

## 2.1 Consumer problem
- Consumers want visible results in skin, body shape, and attractiveness but face low-trust products.
- The market is noisy, claims are often exaggerated, and compliance is weak.
- Existing brands are frequently either:
  - beauty-only (weak body-performance credibility), or
  - sports-only (weak aesthetics/grooming positioning).

## 2.2 Opportunity
Build a **single trusted brand** across beauty and body performance with:
- science-backed formulations,
- measurable routines,
- transparent quality standards,
- compliant claims.

This creates a high-LTV portfolio across daily use categories.

## 6) Regulatory and Compliance Plan

## 6.1 Compliance stance
Operate as a **compliance-forward brand**:
- no disease treatment claims,
- no disguised drug positioning,
- strict label and adverse-event governance,
- robust supplier qualification and batch documentation.

## 6.2 Regulatory difficulty by category
- Topical cosmetics: **Medium** (labeling + safety + post-market obligations).
- Dietary/sports supplements: **Medium-High** (claims, cGMP, ingredient scrutiny).
- Injectable/gray peptide models: **Very High** legal/regulatory risk (excluded from plan).

## 6.3 Region-specific priorities
- US: MoCRA cosmetics obligations, supplement claim discipline.
- EU/UK: strict claim substantiation and health-claims frameworks.
- GCC: market-entry approvals and localized labeling.
- India: label and ingredient compliance plus pricing strategy.
